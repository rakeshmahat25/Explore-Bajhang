About Explore Bajhang

Dedicated to showcasing the hidden beauty of Bajhang, Nepal

Our Story

Born from a passion for discovery and a love for our homeland

Explore Bajhang was founded with a simple mission: to share the breathtaking beauty, rich culture, and hidden gems of Bajhang district with the world. What started as a small initiative by local enthusiasts has grown into a comprehensive platform showcasing everything this magnificent region has to offer.

We believe that Bajhang holds some of Nepal's most pristine landscapes, most authentic cultural experiences, and most thrilling adventures. Our team works tirelessly to document, share, and promote these treasures while supporting local communities and sustainable tourism.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable tourism in Bajhang by showcasing its natural beauty, cultural heritage, and adventure opportunities while supporting local communities and preserving our environment for future generations.

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for travelers seeking authentic experiences in Nepal's lesser-explored regions, making Bajhang a recognized name in sustainable and adventure tourism.
